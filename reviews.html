Northline Echo — Night Train Static

  • Standout Track: "Signal on Fourth Street"

Maren Holt — Concrete & Reverb

Analog synthesizer textures and clear melodic hooks balance upbeat synth-pop momentum with reflective lyrics.

  • Standout Track: "Glass Stairwell"
  • Production Style: Crisp drum transients and wide stereo pads
